# Content Guidelines

## Voice

Professional, clear, responsible, operational, respectful, and helpful. Write for B2B decision-makers without sounding aggressive or making outcome guarantees. Use plain Indonesian and idiomatic, human-reviewed English.

## Evidence and claims

Do not invent or imply clients, partners, revenue, employee counts, years in business, awards, offices, business-sector coverage, regulatory status, certifications, service availability, or guaranteed results. Each factual claim needs an approved source and reviewer. Certification pages require issuer, number, dates, document, and verified status; only verified records can appear publicly.

The master brief's proposed service categories, hero copy and industry names are planning inputs, not proof that PAP offers or serves them. Keep these items as editorial candidates until the company document confirms them.

## Placeholder handling

Use explicit editorial tokens such as `[MENUNGGU DATA RESMI]` in CMS drafts and documentation only. Never ship bracketed tokens, fictional demo content, or fake contact links to a public production page. Until information arrives, show a neutral, reviewed empty state or keep the item unpublished.

## Translation and accessibility

Keep ID and EN meaning aligned; have a human editor review both. Avoid machine-generated translations without review. Provide descriptive links, concise headings, and useful alt text; decorative images use empty alt text.
