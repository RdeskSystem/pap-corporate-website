# Brand Guidelines

## Source and current asset

`assets/logo_pap.png` is the supplied canonical PAP mark: a square 3375 × 3375 transparent PNG. It is a symbol mark without a supplied wordmark. Use this exact source; do not redraw, recolor, stretch, crop, or combine its shapes into a new logo. The company name may be typeset separately as text, not presented as a newly invented logo.

## Clear space and sizing

Preserve the full source aspect ratio. Keep clear space around the mark at least 1/4 of its rendered width. Do not render below 24 px for the symbol; use the company name separately in compact contexts. Use a light/white surface when transparency exposes the background; prepare a reviewed dark-surface treatment only if the original mark remains legible without alteration.

## Palette

Use the supplied primary blue `#293696` as the dominant brand color and orange `#FA9F1A` as a restrained accent. Supporting colors: navy `#111827`, slate `#475569`, light slate `#F1F5F9`, white `#FFFFFF`. Do not derive unapproved brand colors from compression/edge pixels of the PNG.

## Incorrect usage

No distortion, non-proportional resizing, effects, shadows on the mark, unapproved recoloring, cropping, rotating, low-resolution enlargement, or placement on a low-contrast background. Do not fabricate a white/dark logo variant by changing the supplied artwork.

## Typography and applications

Use Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans with readable Indonesian/English support. Use blue for primary links and major identity; reserve orange for primary CTA emphasis and small active indicators. Photography, when supplied, should be authentic professional people, business operations and Indonesian corporate environments; avoid aggressive debt-collection imagery and unrelated staged stock. Prefer restrained diagrams for process explanations.

## Derivatives

Proportional favicon, Apple touch icon, PWA/maskable icons, and OG/social previews are generated by `npm run assets:generate` from the canonical source. Horizontal/mobile lockups use the original symbol beside separately typeset company-name text; no wordmark artwork is fabricated. Review transparency and legibility at each target size before production release.
